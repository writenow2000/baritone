Baritone

Listen to any PDF

A deep, clear American voice reads your documents aloud. The neural voice runs entirely on this phone — nothing is uploaded.

The first time, the voice model (about 90 MB) downloads once and is then stored for offline use.

Voice

Depth
Lowers the pitch of the voice. "Deep" keeps diction crisp; "Deepest" is very low.
Pauses at punctuation
Commas, semicolons, sentence ends and paragraph breaks each get their own pause length.
Voice engine
CPU works on every iPhone. GPU can be faster on recent iPhones (iOS 26+) but downloads a larger model. Changing this reloads the voice.

Baritone is open source (MIT). Voice: Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0), on-device. PDF text: pdf.js.
